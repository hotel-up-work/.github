# 🏨 Hotel Up Work

**Hotel Up Work** is a hospitality technology organization building modern software for hotels, apart-hotels, villas, guest houses, and other accommodation businesses.

Our goal is to simplify hotel operations by bringing reservations, guests, payments, housekeeping, direct bookings, automation, analytics, and communication into one connected ecosystem.

We focus on practical tools that help hotels reduce manual work, improve guest experience, increase direct bookings, and manage everyday operations from a single platform.

🌐 [hotelup.work](https://hotelup.work)

## 🚀 What we build

Hotel Up Work develops software focused on real hotel operations:

* Hotel Property Management System (PMS)
* Reservation and availability management
* Guest CRM and stay history
* Direct booking engine
* Hotel websites and booking pages
* Payments and balance tracking
* Housekeeping management

* Hotel dashboards and analytics

## 🏨 HotelOS

Our main product is **HotelOS** — a unified operating system for modern hotels.

HotelOS is designed to connect the most important parts of hotel management in one simple system:

**Website → Booking → Reservation → Guest → Payment → Stay → Housekeeping → Communication → Analytics**

Instead of using many disconnected tools, hotels can manage their daily operations from one platform.

## 📅 Reservations & availability

HotelOS provides a centralized reservation system for managing:

* Room availability
* Reservations
* Check-in and check-out
* Booking dates
* Room changes
* Reservation statuses
* Guest information
* Booking sources
* Direct and external bookings

The goal is to always provide hotel staff with a clear view of what is happening across the property.

## 👥 Guest CRM

Every guest can have a complete profile containing:

* Contact information
* Reservation history
* Previous stays
* Payments
* Preferences
* Notes
* Communication history
* Returning guest information

This allows hotels to build better relationships with guests instead of treating every booking as an isolated reservation.

## 🌐 Direct booking

Hotels can receive reservations directly through their own website or booking page.

Direct booking helps hotels:

* Reduce dependency on external booking platforms
* Reduce commission costs
* Own their guest relationships
* Track marketing sources
* Build stronger hotel brands

Bookings made through Hotel Up Work are connected directly with HotelOS.

## 💳 Payments

HotelOS helps hotels track financial information related to reservations:

* Reservation totals
* Deposits
* Remaining balances
* Payment history
* Refunds
* Payment statuses
* Unpaid reservations

The platform can also integrate with external payment providers and accounting services.

## 🧹 Housekeeping

Housekeeping tools help hotel teams manage room preparation and cleaning workflows.

Features can include:

* Room cleaning status
* Cleaning assignments
* Ready / dirty / cleaning states
* Checkout-triggered tasks
* Staff responsibilities
* Room preparation tracking

## 💬 Guest communication

Hotel Up Work helps automate communication throughout the guest journey.

Examples include:

* Booking confirmations
* Pre-arrival instructions
* Check-in information
* Stay reminders
* Checkout messages
* Payment reminders
* Post-stay communication

Communication can be connected with email, SMS, messengers, and other services.

## 📊 Analytics

Hotel owners and managers can understand hotel performance through dashboards and reports.

Analytics can include:

* Occupancy
* Revenue
* Arrivals
* Departures
* Reservations
* Outstanding balances
* Booking sources
* Direct vs external bookings
* Returning guests
* Room performance

## 🤖 Hotel AI

Hotel Up Work is also exploring AI-powered hotel management.

Hotel staff should be able to ask questions such as:

* What rooms are available this weekend?
* Who is arriving today?
* Which reservations are unpaid?
* What rooms still need cleaning?
* Which booking source generates the most revenue?
* Who are our most valuable returning guests?

AI can work as an additional interface on top of HotelOS data and operations.
